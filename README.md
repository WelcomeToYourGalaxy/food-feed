# food-feed

A live wire on the food and drink industries worldwide: who owns the food
system, who funds what is said about it, what it does to the people eating it
and the land it runs on, and what the label actually certifies.

Built after "The Food and Drink Industries" on Welcome to Your Galaxy — which
that page places under Suppression by "Representation" rather than under
control of resources, because these companies sit between a body and its food
rather than upstream of it.

Organic gets two subjects because the section takes it apart at length. One
follows the certification itself — paperwork rather than testing, each
certifying agent covering hundreds of operations, a small share tested
annually, a certifier paid out of the crop it approves, and hundreds of
operations decertified every year anyway. The other follows what is actually
found: residues in certified produce, residue limits, and the monitoring
programmes meant to catch them.

## The gate

The food pages are refused, and they are most food coverage: recipes,
restaurant reviews, taste tests, menu launches, food festivals, diet plans.
So is business coverage with no influence or health angle.

A story no subject will claim is refused and counted as refused, rather than
filed under a fallback subject it did not earn.

## Weight

A decision (2), institutional material (2), a measured figure (1), a pending
decision with a date (1), a named jurisdiction (1), a primary source (1). At
three or more it is marked consequential.
